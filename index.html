About

I am a PhD candidate at the DHLab, KNAW Humanities Cluster, working on the TRIFECTA project under the supervision of Dr. Marieke van Erp.

I am interested in how language change may reflect social and cultural change. I work on diachronic semantic change detection.

Before the PhD, I obtained an MSc in Digital Humanities and an MA in Philosophy from KU Leuven.
